# Code of Conduct

We want Hisaab to be a welcoming project. Be respectful, assume good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and do not harass or discriminate. Do not share other people's financial or personal documents.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that violate this, and may ban repeat offenders. This is a short summary; projects may adopt the full Contributor Covenant text if preferred.
